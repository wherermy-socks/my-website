Letters to the Editor


Felt like I had to get the first one out of the way [11/7/2026]

Like the title says; I didn't really think this small first essay through a whole lot, but after a struggle-fest with placing webrings on my page, I unfortunately came to ponder if I really wanted webrings, which led me to realize I don't, and then made me rethink every little custom I've seen in the indie web space.

Before someone calls me a quitter, I just want to clarify that I've been thinking about the broader...culture? way of life? traditions? here on Nekoweb since starting my blog back in September. I also have a sleep deprived manifesto written on my phone's Notes app at 12AM, so as you can see, a very scholarly take that didn't suddenly sprout from rage-quitting webrings. If you've cared to notice, I do have one for astronomy. Props to them for having simpler code I could understand.

This truly started while scrolling the Nekoweb 'Explore' page. I tend to visit websites sporadically and read their blogs at 1AM on my bed. As I did this, I came across someone who questioned their own motivations for updating, "knowing no one would read it." Which, rude. I'm someone, and I'm reading it. On a more serious note, it made me pause and ponder the general social etiquette(?) on here. I don't even know if to use the word etiquette.

Isn't it fascinating that the indie web space promotes so many small things that aid community building - webrings, guestbooks, buttons - and yet people feel alone? That their blog is basically "the void"? And I'm sure the metrics added by Nekoweb - # of followers and views - don't help this sentiment. And as the time investment on a blog wildly differs based on the person, I'm sure the way each person builds relationships through it is vastly different too. Much like social media, what one person considers to be their only way of socializing, another checks once a month if they remember it exists.

I don't really have a conclusion to this. I've got this bittersweet feeling that maybe the customs leftover from pre-modern internet times aren't that well-directed to the new generation of indie website users and how they connect, but I can't posit a solution that doesn't devolve into a social media rant...I guess I'll just bother people in their guestsbooks until "the void" becomes synonymous with Socks >:].
