Who am I?

Hello! I go by Socks. I mainly made this blog as a hobby to replace scrolling on other platforms. I only use FB for local events, Reddit for questions and YT for deep dives; all are hacked to prevent scrolling.

What can you find here?

The site is divided into basic sections in the sidebar. These include:

  • Intro

    • This "about me" page; "code journey" which covers everything I do to this blog and therefore what I learn 'cause it's all new; and my guestbook!

  • AUs / Originals

    • I'm really into creative writing and worldbuilding AUs I will never finish. The formats for all of these might differ; actual writing, graphic mind-maps, character files and even multimedia collages. Some will be about actual published media, while others might include original OCs. Some things might edge 18+ but no actual explicit content will pop-up!

  • Non-fiction (Not included yet!)

    • I like research. Right now, I'm not writing any papers as I'm finishing university and looking for a new job. However, current interests involve academic takes on fandom and fandom-adjacent ventures.

  • Self-Learning

    • A section for me to post mini-snippets of what I'm currently teaching myself (outside of web design lol). I'm a hobby jumper so aside from stuff I do in real life (salsa classes, blackout poetry, Snoopy word soups, reading), I'm setting aside a space on here for things I'm interested in that I want to track! Tabs right now include: mathematics 'cause I am an Economics student (might delve into statistics too), roller skating which I'm pretty far along in but I'm aiming for more tricks, and bomba, a traditional dance that's part of Puerto Rico's culture! Future projects I want to indulge in and might publish here: animate MMDs (or learn blender?) and creating a text-adventure website.

  • Commonplace Book (Not included yet!)

    • I've tried a physical and tablet version of this but I always fail to maintain it. Hopefully posting it on here will work. It'll probably be a compendium of quotes that I compilate from books, movies, videos, etc.
