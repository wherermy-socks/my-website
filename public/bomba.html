Bomba! A Puerto Rican tradition.

To start off this section, I want to include an article about bomba on the island and some of its main features: The African Roots of Bomba y Plena in Puerto Rico.

How am I learning bomba?

Not gonna lie, I'm just winging it. I've started by trying to learn the different bomba rhythms and identifying them by ear. If you read the article, there's A LOT. Because of this, I've zeroed in on getting down the basic rhythms of:

  • Sicá
  • Yubá
  • Cuembé
  • Holandé
  • Seis corrido

This video explains the different rhythms and the basic steps that go with them. They speak a bit about the cultural significance and etiquette that goes with it, so an educational watch all around! Even though I'm Puerto Rican born and raised, and live on the island with pretty accessible bomba classes around, I'm trying not to sink money into lots of hobbies so we're doing our best with what we've got. Thankfully, a great community of bomba enthusiasts have tried to make it available online.

This playlist actually has a ton of different rhythms with different BPM that are made to train your ear!
